# Community code of conduct

VitalBreathe welcomes riders and contributors of every background and level of
technical experience. Be kind, practical, and honest about what has and has not
been tested.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss the idea or evidence, not the person.
- Give newcomers enough context to participate safely.
- Respect privacy. Never repost another person's ride, health, location,
  account, Bluetooth, or sensor data.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and relationships with relevant vendors.
- Treat physical-device testing as safety-sensitive work. Do not encourage
  interaction with a Karoo while someone should be watching the road.
- Accept a maintainer's decision to pause a feature until it can be tested
  responsibly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate
misinformation, doxxing, publishing private diagnostic data, or disrupting
another person's device or account are not accepted.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces where someone represents the VitalBreathe project. Maintainers may edit
or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently block participation when needed to protect the community.

For an ordinary disagreement, keep the discussion on the relevant issue or
pull request. For a private conduct or safety concern, use GitHub's
[private report form](https://github.com/misb74/vitalbreathe-karoo/security/advisories/new)
and state clearly that it is a conduct report. If the concern involves the
project maintainer, use GitHub's own abuse-reporting process instead.
