# VitalBreathe 1.0.0-beta.1

VitalBreathe’s first public beta brings live Tymewear VitalPro breathing data to Hammerhead Karoo. It combines a ride-ready dashboard, a five-minute ventilation graph, personal ventilation zones, resilient Bluetooth recovery, and fresh breathing data in FIT activities.

This is an independent community project. It is not produced, supported, or endorsed by Tymewear, Hammerhead, SRAM, Garmin, or their affiliates.
Treat its readings as training information, not medical advice.

## Install or upgrade

Download `vitalbreathe-karoo-1.0.0-beta.1.apk` from the assets below. On a latest-generation Karoo, share the APK from your phone to the Hammerhead Companion app, accept the installation on the Karoo, then open VitalBreathe once to grant sensor access. Pair the strap through **Settings → Sensors → Add Sensor → Extensions**.

If you have the signed `1.0.0-personal.6` test build, Beta.1 can upgrade it in place because the Android package and signing identity are unchanged. A CI or locally debug-signed build uses a different identity and must be uninstalled first; uninstalling clears VitalBreathe settings and its Karoo sensor pairing.

This release is intentionally marked **Latest**, rather than GitHub prerelease, because Karoo resolves its in-app update manifest through GitHub’s `releases/latest` address. The software itself remains a beta until the published validation programme is complete.

## Highlights

- A combined dashboard shows VE30, zone, breathing rate, tidal volume, Karoo heart rate, and VitalPro battery.
- The full-page graph keeps five minutes of VE history, offers 15s, 30s, and 60s smoothing, and leaves honest gaps when the sensor drops out.
- The standalone VE field offers live, 5s, 15s, 30s, and 60s views.
- Five personal ventilation thresholds can drive consistent dashboard colours, graph bands, live zone time, and FIT zone records. Leaving every threshold blank safely disables zones.
- FIT output pauses when breathing data becomes stale and resumes without zero-filling or backfilling the outage.
- Bluetooth ownership, bounded setup, reconnect backoff, a valid-breath watchdog, and visible diagnostics make failures easier to recover from and understand.
- The app has no Internet permission and does not sign in to or upload data to Tymewear.

## Validation position

The previous signed test build was physically checked on a latest-generation Karoo with a VitalPro. The dashboard and graph rendered during a real ride. Two controlled connection losses recovered in about 11 and 13 seconds without an app restart or re-pair. Strict FIT decoding after the metadata-order correction found every custom field described before use, and the checked activity contained 65 populated breathing records without zero, partial, or stale core values.

Beta.1 preserves that runtime foundation while adding public packaging, attribution, privacy-safe diagnostics, and the Karoo update path. The exact current status and evidence boundaries are recorded in [`docs/VALIDATION.md`](https://github.com/misb74/vitalbreathe-karoo/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md).

Still outstanding are configured-zone and experimental-MI hardware checks, cold pairing of the final beta, Karoo sleep/wake, official-app contention, third-party FIT imports, 20 consecutive rides, one four-hour outdoor ride, and Karoo 2 validation.

## Verify the download

The release includes eight assets. The publishing command rebuilds and verifies
the signed APK immediately before uploading them:

- the signed APK;
- its SHA-256 checksum;
- the signer certificate fingerprint;
- exact source and build information;
- the Karoo update manifest and icon; and
- VitalBreathe’s third-party notices and the vendored SDK’s Apache 2.0 licence.

On macOS:

```bash
shasum -a 256 -c vitalbreathe-karoo-1.0.0-beta.1.apk.sha256
```

On Linux:

```bash
sha256sum -c vitalbreathe-karoo-1.0.0-beta.1.apk.sha256
```

The expected signing certificate is published in `SIGNER_CERT_SHA256.txt`. Android also enforces the same signer for an in-place upgrade.

## Known limits and support

VitalBreathe does not import threshold tests, provide a Tymewear account connection, run a threshold test, or upload activities to the Tymewear cloud. Some analysis services preserve FIT developer fields without displaying them.

Use the project’s [support guide](https://github.com/misb74/vitalbreathe-karoo/blob/main/SUPPORT.md) for setup and troubleshooting. Do not attach raw FIT files, full diagnostic captures, routes, Bluetooth addresses, sensor IDs, or private health information to a public issue. Security-sensitive reports belong in [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/misb74/vitalbreathe-karoo/security/advisories/new).

For the complete change history, see the [changelog](https://github.com/misb74/vitalbreathe-karoo/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
